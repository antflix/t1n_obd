Connecting to scanner…
Select measurements to load saved SD records.

Saved SD records only. Recent recording is adaptive: at least 10 seconds between changed values; unchanged values may be recorded 30 minutes apart. Older records show min/average/max summaries. Gaps are not filled.

Alerts and data availability

Vehicle alert rules and persistent alert history are not available in this firmware. Missing readings do not establish vehicle health. View verified faults in the diagnostic WebUI.

Modules

Viewing a module does not connect to it or change the diagnostic session. A module without a successful scan has unknown fault status.

System

This display is read-only. Logging and diagnostic sessions are controlled by the ESP32. Absolute time cannot survive power loss without resynchronization or a battery-backed clock.
